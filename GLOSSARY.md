# Lattely

Lattely helps people discover highly rated cafés around a chosen location in Barcelona.

## Language

**Barri**:
One of Barcelona’s 73 official neighborhoods, offered as a named search-center choice.
_Avoid_: District, borough

**Barri center**:
An approximate central point used as the search center for a selected barri. The surrounding search radius may include cafés in neighboring barris.

**Search center**:
The geographic point from which café distances and the search radius are measured.

**Search radius**:
The maximum straight-line distance from the search center within which cafés are included. “Anywhere” includes the entire Barcelona demo café dataset.
